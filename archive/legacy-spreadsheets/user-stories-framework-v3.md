## Key Terms & Sources

| DTG RAHP – Key Terms & Source References  \|  Definitions grounded in VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3 and DTGWG documentation | Column 2 | Column 3 | Column 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Term | Definition | Source | URL |
| Decentralized Trust Graph (DTG) | A graph of cryptographically verifiable trust relationships between people, groups, organisations, and AI agents, where no centralised database exists and all parties control their own portable subgraph. | ToIP / DIF DTGWG Charter, Sept 2025 | https://lf-toip.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/HOME/pages/257785857/ |
| Verifiable Trust Community (VTC) | A secure online group where members' identities are verified, relationships between members are cryptographically verifiable, and the community has governance policies for membership. The DTG bootstrapping draft uses a Linux Kernel Maintainer Verification scenario as its reference example. | VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3, Jan 2026 | https://github.com/trustoverip/dtgwg-cred-tf |
| Initiator | The first person who takes the initial steps to establish a new VTC, automatically becoming the first member and setting initial policies for who can join. Initiator authority should be time-bounded. | VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3 |  |
| Community DID (C-DID) | A Decentralized Identifier generated to identify the VTC itself. Issued by the initiator during Phase 1 genesis. | VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3 |  |
| Membership DID (M-DID) | A Decentralized Identifier used by each individual member to represent their membership in a specific VTC. Members may hold multiple M-DIDs for different VTCs. | VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3 |  |
| Verifiable Trust Agent (VTA) | A cloud-hosted agent representing the VTC that enforces policy via a Policy Enforcement Point (PEP), issues VMCs, and writes records to the trust registry. | VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3 |  |
| Verifiable Membership Credential (VMC) | A verifiable credential issued by the VTA (using the C-DID as issuer) to a member's M-DID, confirming their membership in the VTC and their role. | VTC Bootstrapping Draft v3 |  |
| Verifiable Relationship Credential (VRC) | A peer-to-peer credential exchanged between two parties to cryptographically prove their real-world relationship and mutual trust.
