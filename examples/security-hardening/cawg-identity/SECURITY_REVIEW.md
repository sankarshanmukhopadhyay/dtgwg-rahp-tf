**Attack / failure path**

A crafted URI causes a validator to access an internal, local, redirecting, or attacker-controlled resource during identity validation.

**Existing mitigations**

- Cryptographic integrity and signature validation constrain direct content tampering but do not close this higher-layer failure path.

**Residual gap**

The trust model requires network-aware resolution but URI scheme, redirect and destination restrictions can vary by implementation.

**Recommended hardening**

Specify allowed schemes, redirect limits, private-address blocking, size/time limits and offline behavior; publish SSRF-focused conformance vectors.

**Verification / closure evidence**

- Add a negative interoperability vector reproducing the failure and require a distinct non-success validation result.

- Cryptographic integrity and signature validation constrain direct content tampering but do not close this higher-layer failure path.

Cryptographically or normatively bind entity_id namespace/issuer provenance and reject ambiguous cross-CA reuse.
