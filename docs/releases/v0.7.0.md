# v0.7.0 — Composition and Situational Assurance

v0.7 turns the CAWG/C2PA deployment from a portability demonstration into a continuously reviewable multi-specification assurance environment.

## Highlights

- adds a 36-scenario CAWG/C2PA pressure-test corpus;
- independently reviews the Identity governance, VC/VP and vLEI experiments plus Organizational Identity 1.1;
- adds five cross-specification composition pressure tests;
- adds CAWG-specific security-hardening reviews and combined RAHP + security syntheses;
- expands the CAWG-local risk register from 13 to 28 risks;
- introduces deployment-neutral selected upstream-issue watching for architecture/governance discussions that can change the risk picture before normative text lands, used independently by CAWG/C2PA and DTG;
- adds a machine-readable and rendered mandate-readiness register;
- fixes Pages link validation for `/rahp-toolkit/` base-URL links, renders the CAWG mandate-readiness canonical YAML through the structured-data projection, repairs CAWG assessment-pack links, and adds regression coverage.

The CAWG/C2PA deployment remains observational. Upstream projects retain all specification, conformance and governance authority.
